// FAQ

Questions
we get
a lot.

Didn't find your answer? Just email us — Dinesh reads every one personally.

Ask Us Directly →
How long does a project take? +
Websites: 1–4 weeks. A simple landing page? 3–5 days. Mobile apps: 6–12 weeks depending on scope. We give you a real timeline upfront — and we stick to it.
What's your payment structure? +
50% upfront to begin, 50% on delivery. For larger projects, milestone-based payments are available. UPI, bank transfer, and international payments all accepted.
Do you work with international clients? +
Absolutely. We work with clients across India and globally via WhatsApp, email, Zoom. Time zones haven't stopped us yet — and our work speaks for itself regardless of location.
What if I only have a rough idea? +
Perfect — that's the ideal starting point. We're excellent at taking vague concepts and shaping them into clear briefs. Just reach out and we'll figure it out together, no pressure.
What if you're not the right fit for us? +
We'll say so honestly — and point you toward someone who is. We'd rather you find the right team than take a project we can't do justice to. That's just how we operate.
Do you offer ongoing support? +
Yes — 2 weeks of free post-launch support for bug fixes. Retainer packages for ongoing maintenance, updates, and new feature development are also available.
